## Context

The legacy site was written in New Zealand English ("catalogue", "colour"). The home-route copy grilling (`e2805adefd47`, Q9) asked whether the restored copy keeps that spelling; the operator chose American English for the whole site.

## Decision

Visitor-facing copy uses American English spelling on every route: catalog, color, center, organize, license. A legacy sentence restored verbatim gets its spelling changed and nothing else; the verdict quotes the American form. Legacy proper nouns and quoted material (a testimonial, a story author's own text) keep their author's spelling.

## Consequences

Every copy verdict on every route applies this. A `regression` or `sanctioned` item whose target wording carries a British spelling is a mistake to catch on review, like a wrong rule citation. Code identifiers, agent prose and the kipu store are not covered; they follow CLAUDE.md's conventions.
