## Context

Rule 1 of [[2032533fbe92]] swaps "Grid Kit" to "grid beam" in body copy. The two newsletters (2021 winter, 2022) and "Building with Grid Kit" are the startup's own posts, written about the company at the time; the first port kept the name there and added an editorial note explaining it. The stories grilling (`e2805adefd47`, P3) judged the note.

## Decision

A historical post keeps "Grid Kit" wherever it appears, with no editorial note, no "originally on gridkit.nz" byline and no publish date on the page: the text is the record, as legacy published it. The three guides (What's a Grid Unit, How to install furniture bolts, How to mark and cut grid beams) are reference material, not history, and take the rule 1 swap.

## Consequences

Nobody re-swaps the newsletters or "Building with Grid Kit" under rule 1. A future story that reports on the startup's past is treated the same way.
