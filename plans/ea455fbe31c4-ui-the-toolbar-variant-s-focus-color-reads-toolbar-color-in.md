The `toolbar` Button variant's own `_focus: { color: 'gray.700' }` (`../ui/src/components/Button.tsx:120`) reads the recipe's `recipes` cascade layer as a hardcoded literal; it never reads the `--toolbar-color` custom property the variant's base `color` reads (`Button.tsx:112`, `var(--toolbar-color, {colors.gray.700})`), the variable [[b574a94092bf]] taught the sandbox's off toggles to set. A real mouse click focuses the clicked button (Chromium's default), so after that slice's fix, an off toggle that is clicked and then left alone (mouse moved away, no further interaction) still reads `gray.700`, matching the toggle's ON appearance, until something else takes focus; only a hover or a blur reads the correct `gray.400`. [[014c146699e5]] records the reading. Closes [[014c146699e5]] (interaction, `regression`) on `/designs/bed-frame`.

## Outcome

Shipped as the plan's fallback shape, not its candidate: a second variable scoped to focus. In `../ui` (commit 748aeb5 on its `main` over 1c6a07b, by pathspec: `src/components/Button.tsx` and `CHANGELOG.md`), the `toolbar` variant's `_focus` color reads `var(--toolbar-focus-color, {colors.gray.700})` in place of the literal `gray.700` (`Button.tsx:123`), with a comment saying why and a sentence on the CHANGELOG's mobile menu toggle entry naming the variable. In `../gridkit` (commit 656bf68 on its `main` over bf58a1f, by pathspec: `core/sandbox/src/controls/index.tsx` alone), the two off toggles set `--toolbar-focus-color: colors.gray.400` beside `--toolbar-color`, the `NavHeader` form, with `_focus: {}` kept as legacy's line (plan b574a94092bf's call) and the comment extended. No site file changes; the fix is seen here through the two uncommitted overrides at once (the sandbox tarball packed from `../gridkit` after `build:pkg`, and `file:../ui` with `transpilePackages: ['@villagekit/ui']`), reverted by path before this commit.

Why the fallback and not the candidate. The candidate (the `_focus` color reading `--toolbar-color`) would have moved the nav toggle's focus color too, and the live legacy site says the nav toggle's focus color is not its rest color: `audit/_probeea45/focus-probe.mjs` against `https://gridkit-landing-villagekit.vercel.app` (`legacy.json`) reads the header's `Toggle menu` at 375 at `rgb(23, 25, 35)` (`gray.900`) at rest and `rgb(45, 55, 72)` (`gray.700`) under keyboard focus, the variant's own focus color winning over the `sx` rest color by specificity in Chakra v2, so `--toolbar-color: gray.900` at focus would have been a new regression on `shell`. The sandbox's on toggles and the `Reset view` button read `gray.700` under keyboard focus on both sides, and the off toggle reads `gray.400` on legacy under a click (pointer moved away, still focused), under keyboard focus and once blurred. The fallback keeps the variant's semantics legacy's for every consumer that sets nothing (focus is `gray.700`, independent of rest), and translates the sandbox's `_focus: {}` opt-out, which in v2 erased the variant's focus mapping so focus fell to the rest color, into the one write that does the same in v3. A ui-only alternative (the nav toggle setting `--toolbar-focus-color: colors.gray.700` while the recipe's `_focus` fell through `--toolbar-focus-color` to `--toolbar-color`) would have avoided the `../gridkit` edit at the price of a variant whose default focus color follows its rest color, which is not the legacy variant; rejected for that. The plan's "Not this slice: the sandbox's own two lines" is therefore deviated on: the fallback shape needs the consumer to set the second variable, and those two lines are the consumer.

Verified on `pnpm dev` under both overrides, `audit/_probeea45/focus-probe.mjs http://localhost:3000`: before the change (`before.json`) the off auto-rotate toggle at 1280 read `rgb(45, 55, 72)` at rest while still focused after a click and under keyboard focus; after (`after.json`) it reads `rgb(160, 174, 192)` in both, and `rgb(213, 63, 140)` under the pointer after the click, `rgb(160, 174, 192)` once blurred, the legacy readings; the on toggle at rest, under keyboard focus and at rest while focused after a second click, the `Reset view` button under keyboard focus, and the nav toggle at rest, under keyboard focus, under the pointer after a click and once blurred read the same before and after and the same as legacy. The other `toolbar`-variant consumers (`Zoom in`, `Zoom out`, the fullscreen button, the grid toggle while on, and the site's own `Clear search` on `/designs`, `app/_components/catalogue/SearchBar.tsx:37`) set no variable, so they hold by construction at the `gray.700` fallback, legacy's literal; the Parity review's `review-probe.mjs` (`review-legacy.json` beside `review-current.json`) then read both off toggles, the on toggles, `Zoom in`, `Reset view`, `Clear search` and the nav toggle at 1280 and 375 in every state, matching legacy line for line. `audit/_probeea45/focus-screenshot.mjs` captured the off toggle at rest while focused on both sides at 1280 and 375 (`{legacy,current}-{1280,375}-off-focused.png`), looked at: the same light gray on both.
