What shipped: the viewer container's selector reads `'&:hover, &:focus-within': { '& .sandbox-controls': { opacity: 1 } }`, the form taken of the plan's two. Chakra v3's `css` reads a key as a selector only when it holds `&`, starts with `@` or `_`, or names a condition (`node_modules/@chakra-ui/react/dist/esm/styled-system/conditions.js:5,14`, `SPECIAL_KEY_REGEX` and `has`), so the bare `':hover, :focus-within'` and its nested `'.sandbox-controls'` were flattened into properties, emitting the two empty rules `.css-1kl79jw .sandbox-controls {}` and `.css-1kl79jw .sandbox-controls opacity {}` (the before probe's `rules`) and Emotion's kebab-case error. The raw selector and not the two conditions because Chakra v3's `hover` condition is `["@media (hover: hover)", "&:is(:hover, [data-hover]):not(:disabled, [data-disabled])"]` (`preset-base.js:67-70`), a media guard legacy's rule did not carry, recorded on `shell` as [[53f8a05a03fa]] (open, the operator's), which the condition form would have added a new instance of on this route: a touch screen's sticky hover raises the toolbar on legacy, and the raw form keeps that.
